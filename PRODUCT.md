# Product

## Users

Friend groups, visitors, and teams deciding what to do nearby. Hackathon judges need a complete path they can understand and try quickly.

## Purpose

Encore turns explicitly stated group tastes and practical limits into a local social plan. It helps a group move from scattered suggestions to a shortlist, vote, and revision.

## Positioning

**Group chats do not fail because nobody has ideas. They fail because every idea represents a different kind of night. Encore finds the overlap.**

## Boundaries

- Encore plans local social outings that end in a place decision.
- Discord context is opt-in and capped at the latest 25 messages.
- Raw messages are discarded after explicit signals and limits are extracted.
- Qloo grounds production recommendations. A Qloo failure is visible and does not trigger generic recommendations.
- Opening hours, availability, prices, and bookings remain unconfirmed without supplied facts.

## Brand commitments

- Product name: Encore.
- Direct, concise copy with no em dashes.
- Square controls and editorial typography.
- A judge understands the thesis, privacy behavior, and Qloo role in the first view.
