PRIVACY

Raw chat does not
become a profile.

Encore reads channel messages only when someone runs /encore with use_recent_chat enabled. It requests at most the 25 most recent messages, retains explicitly stated tastes and limits, and discards raw messages immediately after extraction.

Plans contain the request, resolved location, retained signals, limits, options, and votes. They expire after seven days. Avoid putting sensitive information in a plan.

When Qloo powers a plan, Encore sends resolved cultural anchors and location data to Qloo. It does not send raw Discord messages to Qloo.

Encore does not book places or confirm hours, availability, or price. Questions: project repository.
