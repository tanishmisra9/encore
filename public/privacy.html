Privacy at Encore

Encore reads channel messages only when someone runs /encore with use_recent_chat enabled. It requests at most the 25 most recent messages in that channel, extracts explicitly stated tastes and limits, and discards the raw messages. It does not monitor the channel afterward.

Plans contain the show, venue, stated tastes, limits, options, and votes. They remain available by link for seven days. Expired plans are deleted during daily cleanup. Anyone with a web demo plan link can view and change that demo plan. Avoid putting sensitive information in a plan.

During development, Encore may use OpenAI to rank provisional place data. The public web demo uses fictional places until Qloo access is verified. When Qloo powers a plan, Encore sends the artist and venue location to Qloo. It never sends raw Discord messages to Qloo.

Encore does not book venues or confirm opening hours. Questions: project repository.
